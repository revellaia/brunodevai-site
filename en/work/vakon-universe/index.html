01The idea

A transmedia universe with The Frozen Legacy experience, real-time 3D and narrative, built as a continuous system rather than an isolated page.

Challenge
Create a sci-fi IP that lives in the browser, with no app and no download, and still delivers the feeling of a real world.

02The world

A world without sun. A city sustained by stolen heat. In 2221 the sun vanished and humanity learned to survive in the dark. The Glacial City endures through Lumiet, and the Rift Riders move along the edge between light and darkness.

03Experience

A digital universe built across narrative, 3D and technology.

04Design language

Its own visual universe, transmedia presence, immersive environments, real-time 3D, continuous narrative, characters, elements, equipment and a cinematic glacial identity.
