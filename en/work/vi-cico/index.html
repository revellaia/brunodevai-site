01 Challenge

From event website to event operating system.

Turn a three-day scientific congress into a digital presence able to serve, at the same time, communication, programme, speakers, registration, scientific production and attendee operations, without reducing the event to a landing page.

02 Experience

An ecosystem split between a public portal and an individual environment for each congressist.

03 Public portal

The public experience organises programme, speakers, workshops, forums, scientific works, proceedings, partners, gallery, location and registration.

04 Registration

Registration happens on the portal itself, with an own account and immediate access to the Congressist Area.

05 Congressist experience

The private area brings together profile, credential, agenda, attendance, materials, works and certificates, with authentication and authorisation on the server.

06 Scientific work

A work moves through an editorial flow in the system: submission, screening, review, presentation and publication in the proceedings. A work only becomes public in the published state.

  1. 01Submissiondraft · submitted
  2. 02Screening
  3. 03Reviewrevision requested · resubmitted · accepted · not accepted
  4. 04Presentationscheduled
  5. 05Proceedingspublished

07 Certification

Anyone can check a certificate by its code. The check only states whether it is authentic and its status; it does not expose contact data or allow listing participants.

08 Operations

Operations and data appear only in the system diagram. Administrative screens are never shown.
